Our Mission

Supporting Robbinsville Golf, One Fairway at a Time

Ravens on the Green is the booster club behind the Robbinsville High School Golf Program. Every dollar raised at our Charity Golf Outing goes directly toward equipment, course fees, coaching, and travel for the 2026/27 season.

We're a community of parents, alumni, local businesses, and golf fans who believe every Raven golfer deserves the resources to compete. Join us on the course or become a sponsor — either way, you're keeping the program swinging.
