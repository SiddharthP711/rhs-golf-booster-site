About Us

Who We Are

Ravens on the Green is the official booster club for the Robbinsville High School golf team. We're a group of parents, families, and community members dedicated to giving our student athletes every opportunity to compete, grow, and succeed — on and off the course. As a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, every dollar raised goes directly toward supporting our Ravens golfers.

Where the Money Goes

What We Fund

Every fundraiser and sponsorship goes directly toward these four pillars of the program.

Equipment & Gear

Clubs, bags, and course essentials for every Raven golfer.

Tournaments & Travel

Entry fees and transportation to matches across the state.

Coaching & Training Clinics

Skills clinics and coaching to sharpen every player's game.

Team Apparel & Awards

Uniforms and end-of-season recognition for our golfers.
